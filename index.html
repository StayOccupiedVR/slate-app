How the model works. Pythagorean win percentage, regressed toward .500 and combined with log5. Home field adds about four points. Starters are scored against the league's actual ERA, blending season with last five starts and regressing small samples toward the mean, then converted from runs into win probability. Platoon splits contribute a smaller offensive term.

What it isn't. The model knows nothing about lineups, park factors, defense, bullpen quality, weather, or injuries. When it disagrees sharply with the market, assume the model is wrong — the market has more information than four inputs. Treat it as a sanity check, not a signal.

Moneyline · no-vig · expected value

Find the true number under the book's price.

Enter both sides of a moneyline. This strips the sportsbook's margin to show the fair probability, then checks your estimate against it.

The two prices

Edge is your probability minus the fair probability. EV is the average return per $100 if you repeated the bet forever. Kelly is the bankroll share that maximizes long-run growth — most people size at half Kelly or less, because full Kelly assumes your estimate is exactly right and swings hard when it isn't.

This tool doesn't find edges. It checks the arithmetic on an estimate you supply. If that estimate is a hunch, the output is a hunch with decimal places.

Backtest · point-in-time · calibration

Did the model actually know anything?

Replays past games using only what was knowable that morning, then scores the predictions. Weights re-fit instantly once the data is loaded.

Window

Brier score is the mean squared error of the probabilities. Lower is better; always guessing 50% scores .2500. Skill is how much better than that guess the model does, as a percentage. Log loss punishes confident mistakes harder. Calibration is the one that matters most: when the model says 60%, does that group actually win about 60% of the time?

Fitting overfits. The fit button splits the window chronologically, tunes on the earlier 70%, and reports on the held-out 30%. Trust the test column, not the train column. With fewer than a few hundred games, even that is thin — a fitted weight that improves test log loss by a hair is probably noise.

What's excluded. The platoon term is dropped here because team splits can't be reconstructed as of a past date from this API. The backtest scores team strength, home field, and starters only. League ERA is approximated from runs per game to date, which shifts both sides almost equally and largely cancels.
